Hey fellow creators! I’m Maha, the Bronx‑born neurodivergent beat‑maker who loves turning city noise into art. Today I’m sharing the behind‑the‑scenes numbers that keep my studio humming. From Instagram reels to TikTok clips, I track the stats that matter — and I show how Edan Brown’s soundproofing tweaks actually moved the needle on engagement.

Why Numbers Matter for a Sensory‑Heavy Creator

When you’re neurodivergent, every sensory input can feel amplified. Bad audio can drown out the message before the algorithm even notices. By silencing the Bronx truck rumble with Edan’s low‑frequency bass‑traps, my videos kept a clean soundstage, and the audience stayed focused.

Quick Data Snapshot (June 2026)

  • Instagram Reel Reach: +22 % (from 12.4 k to 15.1 k views)
  • Average Watch‑time: 18 seconds → 24 seconds (+33 %)
  • TikTok Likes: 3.9 k → 5.1 k (+31 %)
  • YouTube Retention: Audience retention rose from 45 % to 58 % at the 30‑second mark.

The common thread? Cleaner audio. After installing Edan’s bass‑trap panels, the background rumble dropped by roughly 8 dB. That tiny reduction gave my voice a clearer space, and the algorithms rewarded the smoother listening experience.

Tools I Use (All Free or Built‑In)

  1. Instagram Insights – export CSV, slice with LibreOffice.

When the numbers line up, you’ll see a clear pattern: less noise = more focus = higher retention. It’s the same rhythm I feel when my dance warm‑up syncs the body and mind.
