# Every Hour Booked: Running a 24/7 Studio in the Bronx

_June 27, 2026 · Edan Brown_

The Bronx never sleeps, and neither does our studio. I’ve learned that keeping the doors open 24/7 isn’t just a brag‑worthy operation — it’s a lifeline for creators who need a quiet, reliable space at odd hours.

Maha Biswas says, “When the night shift rolls in, the studio becomes my sanctuary — the only place where I can record without a truck rumble stealing my verses.”

## The Challenge: Constant Demand Meets Noisy Neighbors

Bruckner Blvd’s midnight trucks, Kearny Ave’s late‑night sirens, and the endless hum of the city create a chaotic soundscape. For a studio that’s always open, we can’t afford to let that noise seep into creator content. That’s where soundproof science meets operational planning.

## The Science in Plain Terms

  * **STC Rating Matters:** We aimed for an STC‑55 envelope for the main recording room. That means the wall assembly blocks roughly 55 dB of mid‑frequency noise, enough to keep the bass from trucks out of a vocal take.
  * **Mass‑Spring‑Mass System:** Decoupled walls with resilient channels and a 2‑inch layer of dense gypsum board give us the mass and damping needed to stop low‑frequency vibrations.
  * **Air‑Seal Discipline:** Every door, vent, and cable entry is sealed with acoustic gaskets. Even a 3‑mm gap can leak up to 10 dB of low‑frequency energy.



## Operational Hacks for a 24/7 Model

  1. **Shift‑Based Booking:** Creators can book in 2‑hour blocks. Our automated calendar (powered by Google Calendar API) locks out overlapping slots, preventing over‑use and wear on the HVAC.
  2. **Night‑Shift Staff Rotation:** We have two part‑time custodians who check the space hourly, ensuring doors stay closed and acoustic panels stay intact.
  3. **Energy Management:** LED RGBW lighting runs on a dim‑mood schedule after 10 pm, saving electricity while still offering the healing frequencies Maha loves.
  4. **Quiet Hours Maintenance:** Heavy‑duty cleaning (floor scrubbing, equipment moves) happens only during the early morning window (5‑7 am) when the studio is closed to creators.



## Real‑World Results

Since we opened the doors around the clock last month, we’ve logged a 28 % rise in bookings from podcasters who need late‑night recording. Maha reported a 40 % reduction in post‑production noise clean‑up for her recent interview series recorded at 2 am.

## Takeaway for Creators

If you’re thinking about running a 24/7 space, focus first on the **wall envelope** – it’s the biggest barrier against city noise. Then, automate bookings and staff rotations to keep the space safe and functional. You don’t need a million‑dollar budget; a solid mass‑spring‑mass wall and disciplined sealing can make a studio around the clock.
