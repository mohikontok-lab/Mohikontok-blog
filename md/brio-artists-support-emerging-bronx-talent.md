# BRIO Artists: How We Support Emerging Bronx Talent

The Bronx is buzzing with creative energy, but the streets can drown out the sound of a new artist’s voice. "A quiet, well‑treated space lets me focus on my choreography and film it without background rumble," says Maha Biswas, dancer and content creator. That's why I’m proud to share how Mohikontok Sound Lab partners with the Bronx Recovery of Inter‑cultural Opportunities (BRIO) program to amplify local talent.

## What BRIO Is and Why It Matters

BRIO provides grants, mentorship, and performance opportunities for emerging artists in the Bronx. The program’s goal is simple: give creators the resources they need to thrive. However, many artists lack access to a proper recording or rehearsal space that can handle the neighborhood’s notorious noise—from Bruckner Boulevard’s truck traffic to the subway’s rumble.

## Our Studio as a Launchpad

When we opened Mohikontok Sound Lab, we built more than a dozen‑track recording environment; we built a **soundproof sanctuary** right on Kearney Ave. The studio’s STC‑45 walls, decoupled floor, and sealed door frames cut ambient noise by over 30 dB, according to our measurements. For a BRIO artist, that means a clean take on a vocal track or a crisp video of a dance piece without the hiss of passing traffic.

## How We Help BRIO Artists

  1. **Free Studio Hours** – BRIO recipients get up to 10 hours of studio time each month, booked via our online portal.
  2. **Technical Coaching** – Our team walks artists through mic placement, gain staging, and basic mixing, demystifying the production process.
  3. **Promotion Boost** – Every session ends with a short video clip we edit and share on the lab’s social channels. Maha often highlights these on Instagram, turning a studio session into a social hook.
  4. **Creative Collaboration** – We host quarterly “Open Mic” events where BRIO artists can showcase finished work to a live audience.



## Real Impact – A Quick Story

Last month, Maha recorded a short spoken‑word piece about the Bronx’s resilience. In our treated room, her voice came through crystal clear, free of the usual street buzz. The clip earned 2,300 views on TikTok in the first 24 hours, and she used the snippet as a teaser for her upcoming choreography series.

## Takeaway for Creators

If you’re an emerging artist in a noisy neighborhood, don’t let the environment dictate your sound. A properly treated space like ours can **increase your content’s perceived quality** , keeping audiences engaged longer and helping you grow your platform.
