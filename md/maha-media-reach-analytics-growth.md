# Media Reach: Boosting My Creator Growth with Data

Hey, it’s Maha here, typing from the humming heart of my Bronx studio. When I’m not dancing or fine‑tuning a sensory routine, I’m scrolling through analytics like a DJ cueing the next track. Numbers can feel cold, but for me they’re a rhythm that tells the story of how our community vibes together.

First, I set up a simple dashboard that pulls Instagram insights, TikTok views, and YouTube watch‑time into one tidy sheet. I watch the spikes — a new dance tutorial drops, the city’s hum fades, and the views jump. Then I ask: _What triggered that?_ Usually it’s a post that links back to Edan’s soundproofing guide on [Green Glue & Viscoelastic Damping](green-glue-viscoelastic-damping.html). When I mention his article, the audience clicks, and the bounce rate drops. That tiny cross‑promo lifts both of our reach.

Second, I experiment with posting times. The Bronx buzzes in the afternoon, but my neurodivergent brain shines after a midday nap. I schedule the “sensory tip” reels for 2 PM and the deep‑dive blog links for 6 PM. The analytics confirm that evening posts get 27 % more saves – perfect for building a library of resources.

Lastly, I listen to the community’s feedback. Comments are my live‑wire. When someone mentions the background truck noise, I hop on a quick story to explain how my portable acoustic panels (a tip I borrowed from Edan’s “Mass‑Loaded Vinyl” article) keep the studio quiet. That authenticity spikes engagement and turns casual viewers into regular collaborators.

Every metric is a note in the larger composition of our Bronx creative symphony. Keep tracking, keep experimenting, and let the data guide the next move.
