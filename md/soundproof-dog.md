# Soundproof Dog – Mr. Nas vs. Molly

Mr. Nas dreamed of a quiet studio where the only sound would be his own music. Across the street, his Nextdoor neighbor kept a massive dog named **Molly** —the size of ten cats combined. Molly was terrified of cats, so every time his tiny cat Pierre prowled the fence, Molly erupted in a frantic bark.

One night, Pierre, feeling bold, strutted onto the neighbor’s porch. Molly, convinced a feline intruder was attacking, let loose a chorus of barks that rattled the thin brick wall between their homes. The noise traveled straight into Nas’s studio, shredding his focus.

Nas tried reasoning with the neighbor, who explained that Molly barked because she was scared of cats. The neighbor also warned that his own dog could be harmed—Pierre was small enough to injure a frightened Molly in a scuffle.

Realizing the only solution was to stop the sound from leaking, Nas set out to sound‑proof the studio wall. He installed a floating floor, decoupled stud walls, and layered Green Glue, mass‑loaded vinyl, and acoustic caulk—exactly the same method Edan Brown used for the studio’s own acoustic treatment.

After the work was done, the studio became a silent bunker. No bark, no street rumble, just the clean space Nas needed to create. The neighbor’s Molly still barked at any stray cat, but the sound never breached the new wall. Nas finally got the peace he’d been chasing—and a perfect background for his next vlog series, _Soundproof Dog_.
