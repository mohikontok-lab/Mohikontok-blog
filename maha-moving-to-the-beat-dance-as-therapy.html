Moving to the Beat: Dance as Therapy

Hey friends, I'm Maha, a Bronx Latina who lives for rhythm and movement. Today I’m sharing how dancing isn’t just art—it’s a therapeutic pulse that steadies my neurodivergent mind.

When the world feels too loud, I turn on my favorite beats and let my body translate the sound into safe, tactile feedback. Simple warm‑up stretches, a few hip rolls, and a quick salsa combo can reset my sensory system, grounding me in the present.

In the lab, I love pairing my moves with the cozy acoustics of our sound‑proofed space. It’s quiet enough to hear my breath, yet vibrant enough for the music to fill the room without echo. If you’re curious about how we achieve that balance, check out Edan Brown’s article on budget‑friendly soundproofing: Soundproofing Tips by Edan. It’s the secret sauce that lets my dance flow without distraction.

Try this: play a song you love, set a timer for five minutes, and move however your body feels. Notice how the rhythm eases anxiety, sharpens focus, and sparks creativity. It’s a reminder that our bodies are the ultimate instruments.
