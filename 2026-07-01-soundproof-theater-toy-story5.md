## Soundproofing the Theater Room

1. **Identify low‑frequency leaks** – use a simple **bass‑trap** panel on the ceiling (Edan’s low‑freq tip).
2. **Seal cracks** – acoustic caulk around doorframes and ventilation.
3. **Mass‑loaded vinyl (MLV)** – line walls for heavyweight low‑freq isolation.
4. **Absorption panels** – foam or mineral wool to tame mid‑range reflections.
5. **Electronic assistance** – integrate a **Lilypad**‑style smart assistant to control lighting, sound levels, and cue playback.
