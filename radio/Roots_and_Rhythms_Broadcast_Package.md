# Mohikontok Sound Lab — Radio Broadcast Package
## "Roots & Rhythms: Bronx to Bengal"

### Station-Agnostic Format (30 min / 60 min)

---

## SHOW CONCEPT

A bilingual (Bangla/English) music program bridging the South Asian diaspora with NYC's multicultural soundscape. Each episode blends Mohikontok's Bangla folk catalog with AI-generated fusion tracks (via Suno) that mash Bengali roots with Bronx-born genres — hip-hop, salsa, jazz, Afrobeat, and lo-fi.

**Target Audience:** First/second-gen South Asian NYers, Caribbean & Latinx communities, college radio listeners, world music enthusiasts.

**Tone:** Warm, community-radio intimate. A recording studio that opens its doors to the neighborhood.

## HOST SCRIPT TEMPLATE (Cold Open + Intro)

> **[Sound of Bronx street ambience — distant traffic, elevated train rumble — crossfades into a Bangla radio being tuned, static giving way to a baul melody. Then: voice.]**

> **HOST (warm, conversational):**  
> *Nomoshkar, New York. As-salamu alaykum. Good evening, family.*  
> *You're tuned into Roots & Rhythms, broadcasting from Mohikontok Sound Lab right here on Kearney Avenue in the Bronx.*  
> *I'm your host, [NAME], and for the next [30/60] minutes, we're going to travel. From the banks of the Padma River to the platform of the 6 train. From the dhol to the 808. From Bengal to the Bronx — and every neighborhood in between that makes this city what it is.*  
> *Tonight: baul folk meets boom-bap. Bengali poetry over dancehall riddims. And a question we're sitting with — what does it sound like when you carry a homeland in your chest and build a life in the South Bronx?*  
> *Stay with us. This is Roots & Rhythms.*
