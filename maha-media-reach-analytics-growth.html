Media Reach: Growing My Audience with Analytics

by Maha Biswas

As a neurodivergent creator from the Bronx, I’ve learned that the numbers behind my posts can be as soothing as a steady beat. In this post I walk you through three simple ways I track and boost my reach, all while keeping my sensory system happy.

1. Listen to the data like a rhythm

Analytics dashboards are my new drum‑machines. I set the refresh rate to a comfortable interval—no constant scrolling that overloads my brain. I watch follower spikes as if they were bass drops, noting which hashtags or video clips cause the biggest “boom.” When I see a pattern, I lock in that energy for the next piece of content.

2. Keep the mix balanced

Just like a balanced mix avoids harsh frequencies, I balance my content types. I blend short reels, carousel posts, and longer IGTV tutorials. I track the engagement ratio: likes + comments versus saves versus shares. If a post leans too heavy on likes but few saves, I tweak the call‑to‑action to invite deeper interaction.

3. Use the right EQ: scheduling

Posting at the right time is like placing a synth pad in the right frequency band. I use the built‑in Instagram Insights to find my audience’s peak hours. I schedule posts in batches during my low‑stimulus evening routine, so I don’t have to scramble in the middle of a creative flow.

These modest steps have already lifted my reach by about 12% in the past month, without adding any stressful hustle.

For more sound‑proofing science, check out Edan Brown’s article on low‑frequency challenges—it’s a perfect complement to keeping both my studio and my online presence “quietly powerful.”

I remember feeling the hum of my laptop's fan syncing with the rhythm of my analytics, a tiny reminder that even tech can be soothing.
